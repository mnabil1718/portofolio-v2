## Overview

Matté is an e-commerce platform to help coffee & matcha enthusiast buy original products without a hassle in real-time. On the other hand, seller/admin could also manage products catalogue and inventory with provided admin features, including sales analytics.

## Goals

My goal is to simplify and streamlined ordering and checkout process of e-commerce platform using real-time update and order tracking. From technical point of view, this project serves as my starting point to learn web sockets and real time update in a practical setting.

## Tech Stack

- Next.js
- Supabase
- Midtrans Payment Gateway
- Resend Email Service
